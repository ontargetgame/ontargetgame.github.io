On Target

Career Premier League goals since 1992, as of .

How to play

  1. You get a target number. Something like 50.
  2. Pick 1 to 5 Premier League players. Anyone who has played since 1992. Their career Premier League goals stay hidden while you pick.
  3. Press Reveal. Their goals are added up. Get as close to the target as you can without going over.
Example · Target 50
Darren Fletcher 23 + Harry Maguire 15 + Phil Neville 9 = 47
3 short → 18 points

Scoring

Exact hit with 1 player50
Exact hit with 2 players40
Exact hit with 3 or more30
1 short20
2 short19
10 short11
20 short1
More than 20 short0
Over the target−1 per goal over, at most −20

So every goal you fall short costs you 1 point. That's for most targets, where you score anywhere within 20. Small targets give you less room (e.g. within 5) and big ones more, so the points drop faster or slower. The range is shown under every target.

Players on 0 goals

  • One player who never scored a Premier League goal is fine.
  • Pick two or more and the round is a Blank: 0 points at best.
  • Goalkeepers who never scored can't be picked.

Ways to play

  • Solo: play 3, 5 or 10 rounds for the highest total. Send a challenge link and a friend gets the same targets.
  • Head-to-head: pass one phone between two players. Same target, secret picks. Best of 5 (most points) or first to 5 round wins.
  • No repeats: an optional hard mode where you can't use the same player twice.
